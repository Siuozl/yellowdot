## A Friendly War That Went Further Than Expected

Yesterday, around 8–9 PM IST, Sentinel and Elementals (EC) had what was supposed to be a friendly war.

The idea behind it was simple. According to both nations, Imperials had been avoiding wars, so EC decided to arrange a friendly match against Sentinel instead.

The war started as a 12vs10, but later became 11v10 according to both sides. The battle lasted for about an hour, and in the end Sentinel came out on top with a convincing win.

**Final Score: 159–25**

Even though it was only meant to be a friendly, things didn't end there.

After the war, an argument broke out between Elementals and their ally, Imperials. EC declined Imperial assistance, and during the discussion Imperial leader Almost reportedly told Emir,

*"EC Major Ally status is revoked, yk the protocol."*

Not long after, EC leader Benler replied by wishing Imperials good luck in what he called their "pointless war" against EE, a conflict that has reportedly been going on for around three years.

Meanwhile, Sentinel didn't rub the victory in. Instead, they told EC they were willing to help them improve so they wouldn't lose by such a margin again. They also said EC has the potential to become one of the strongest armies on BendersMC, even saying they could be the second-best army on the server.

A little later, EC leader Zak announced:

### "Unfortunately, EC × Imperial alliance has been revoked. He doesn't truly sees us as a ally for not helping them in THEIR war. Such a shame."

The news got mixed reactions across the community. Some players felt bad for EC after losing an ally, while others were glad to see them return to neutrality. EC also made it clear that they were neutral before the alliance and would remain neutral now.

The whole situation reminds many of a quote by Mahmoud darwish :



### "The wars will end and the leaders will shake hands..."

At the end of the day, the leaders may move on, but it's usually the regular members who are left dealing with the consequences.

Still, both Sentinel and Elementals agreed on one thing: the war itself was fun. For EC, it also highlighted just how much work is left to strengthen their army for future battles.
