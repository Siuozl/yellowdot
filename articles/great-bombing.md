# The Great Bombing
**Disclaimer:** This story is told from my perspective and may not be completely accurate. More official versions might come later.
## 10:29
Everyone that was online on the server was having fun. A lot of chat games were taking place, and everyone was happy.

## 10:30
I was under a tree, after having flown around with my AirJets, randomly wandering without having anything to do. Suddenly, messages appear in the chat. 
> Death shall rain
Those words are repeated over and over.
Then, the sound of repeated TNT hits everyone’s ears. The chat floods with death messages. This person blew up. That person blew up. Everyone panics. 
People are surprised in the chat. “WHAT?” “WTF?” “HOW?”. 
**I panic and press Escape, then click on Exit to Main Menu.**

## 10:31
I log back on. The message appears again. TNT falls again. The land isn’t being damaged, but we are. Fortunately, I’m covered in Seasonal Armor, but most people aren’t… and they just keep dying. They lose their loot. Disaster has struck  BendersMC.

## 11:00
No one talks about it. They forget. It never happened, to most people, because they’ve just logged on. All the people who were online when the disaster happened are no longer online.
